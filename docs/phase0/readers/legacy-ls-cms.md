## 6. Risks and unknowns

* **Q-47 (unknown):** the production CMS is not this build; its approval and report payloads are unobserved. The new ADM-04/ADM-09 must follow chapter 12 (`importo`, `onlyDelivered`, `sendToCustomer`), not this CMS.
* **Q-22 / CMS-04 (risk, high):** stored XSS reachable from the unauthenticated portal; the surface is listed in §4. Interim CSP on the legacy CMS needs the owner's go-ahead; the new app escapes everything and tests hostile values in all 22 rendered fields plus detail keys and notes.
* **CMS-05 (settled by 12a):** `/mono/api/order/pending` is public (`docs/brief/12a-backend-api-and-security.md:129,1015`); the other reads are ADMIN. Not a CMS fix; recorded for the cut-over risk list.
* **CMS-11 (migration):** legacy notes may hold the literal "Nessuna nota presente" and lost line breaks; treat the literal as empty (chapter 08 rule).
* **CMS-07 (data expectations):** duplicated requests from the legacy UI (notes saved onto the wrong order, several tracking updates, several report e-mails) may exist in production data; no new-app rule, but the migration should not assume one note write per order.
* **CMS-14 / Q-06:** "pending" is a `TEMP` substring in the code; the new model uses a state, and the migration must not rely on the substring alone (12b §3.3 for the real rule).
* **Q-05, Q-17, Q-16/Q-08:** amount meaning and VAT; staff accounts; hosting of `dashboard.` and report recipients — unchanged by this reading.
